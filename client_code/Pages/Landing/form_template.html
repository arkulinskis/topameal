How it works

Three steps. Mom does the thinking.

1

I pick your dinners

Three to seven toppers that share fresh ingredients, mix up the meat, and fit your budget. Don't fancy one? Tap no and I'll find another.

2

You shop one list

Merged and sorted in the order you walk the shop, with the cupboard checks set apart. Tick as you go, or send it to whoever's shopping.

3

Cook the right one first

Fish and mince first, sausages later, tins last. Every dinner gets a cook-by day, big simple steps, and a count of what you'll wash.

The rules I cook by

No choosing

Decision fatigue is real. I suggest, you only ever say no. And if you change your mind, it's one tap to bring it back.

Nothing goes off

Plans run most-perishable first. If something won't last until its night, I'll tell you to freeze it when you get home.

Less waste

Toppers that share the cream, the spinach or the peppers get planned together, so half a bag never dies in the fridge.

Dishes counted

Every recipe tells you what to get out, and that's all you'll wash. Some only need the pan and a spoon.

Price tiers, not prices

Thrifty, comfortable or a little treat. You'll always know roughly what kind of week it is, without a calculator.

Shortcuts welcome

Garlic in a jar, pre-grated cheese, frozen shrimp straight into the pan. Cooking when you're tired should be easy.

Questions

Do I have to pick recipes?

No, and that's the point. I plan the week. If you don't fancy something, tap "No thanks" and I'll suggest something else that still fits the rest of the week.

What if I say no to something by mistake?

It goes to "Set aside" at the bottom of your week. Tap "Yes, this one" and it comes straight back. Say no to the same dinner three times and I'll stop suggesting it (you can bring it back any time).

Why rice, pasta and potatoes?

Because most kitchens already have them, they keep for ages, and anything good from a frying pan tastes great poured over them. You only shop for the topping.

How accurate are the prices?

They are tiers, not prices: thrifty, comfortable or a little treat. Prices change by shop and by week, so I keep it simple.

What about allergies?

Tell me what you don't eat in You (no pork, vegetarian, no nuts, no dairy and so on) and I'll leave those out. Always check labels yourself: I can't see what's in your shop's jars.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads either.

Let me take one thing off your plate.

Dinner. I'll take dinner.
